What is the LQ?
The Location Quotient measures geographic concentration relative to the EBT community size.

• LQ = 1.0: Normal dispersion.
• LQ = 2.0: The note is 2x more frequent in this country than expected.
• LQ = 0.2: The note is 5x rarer than expected.
